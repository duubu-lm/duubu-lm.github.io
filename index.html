Duubu Agency Lead Nurture + Chatbot

TypebotMake.com GPT-4o MiniGHLAirtable

A full lead nurture system triggered by an AI-powered chatbot embedded on the portfolio website. A visitor chats with Typebot, gets scored by GPT-4o Mini, auto-creates a GHL contact with a tag, logs to Airtable, and enters a 3-email nurture sequence. Zero manual work after setup.

01Visitor fills Typebot chatbot → name, email, industry, service, budget, message
02Typebot sends webhook to Make.com on completion
03GPT-4o Mini scores the lead → returns Hot, Warm, or Cold

05Airtable logs all lead data → Name, Email, Industry, Service, Budget, Score

3. 30-day re-engagement tag added automatically after the full nurture sequence ends.

Enterprise AI Recruitment

Google FormsZapier HubSpotGPT-4o Mini

When a candidate submits their application via Google Forms, GPT-4o Mini immediately evaluates their qualifications against the role's requirements — scoring fit across experience, skills, and availability. Qualified applicants receive an automated congratulations email and trigger an HR team notification. Unqualified applicants receive a warm, on-brand rejection that protects candidate experience. Edge cases are flagged for manual HR review, so no strong candidate slips through.

01Candidate submits application via Google Forms
02Zapier webhook passes form data to GPT-4o Mini instantly
03GPT-4o Mini scores fit → Qualified, Borderline, or Unqualified
04Qualified → congratulations email + HR team notification sent
05Unqualified → warm on-brand rejection email sent automatically
06Borderline → flagged for manual HR review, no candidate slips through

⚡ Possible Upgrades

1. Auto-send a Calendly scheduling link to top-tier candidates immediately after the congratulations email.
2. Instant Slack notification to the hiring manager when an A-tier applicant is detected, including a GPT-generated qualification summary.
3. A 30-day re-engagement drip for borderline candidates — held in a pool and contacted when a relevant new opening is posted.

Best For

Small BusinessAutomated screening + response emails for a single job posting
Medium BusinessMulti-role triage, Slack hiring alerts, Calendly interview scheduling
